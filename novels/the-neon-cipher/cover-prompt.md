# Cover Prompt — The Neon Cipher

### Concept
A high-voltage cyber-occult action fantasy cover showing a young athletic male courier standing in a rain-slicked neon-lit Shinjuku alley in Neo-Tokyo at night, with a glowing ancient cybernetic gauntlet on his right arm emitting electric blue lighting and holographic runes.

### Prompt
```
Action fantasy book cover featuring a young athletic male courier with dark hair in a rain-slicked cyberpunk alley in Neo-Tokyo at night, glowing ancient cybernetic metallic gauntlet on his right arm emitting bright electric blue sparks and floating holographic Aetheric runes, neon Japanese signs reflecting on wet asphalt, cinematic lighting, dramatic gritty mood, semi-realistic digital painting, 3:4 aspect ratio, no text on the image.
```

- Color Palette: Electric Blue (#00E5FF), Neon Purple (#D500F9), Wet Asphalt (#0A0E17)
