# Pangkal — Cover Image Prompt

## Concept

Seorang kurir muda berlari di atas jembatan akar yang mati dan abu-abu, membelakangi kota yang menyala di kejauhan. Jaket anyamannya terkoyak, tali serat melingkar di lengannya, dan di pinggangnya menggantung dua tabung — satu bersegel lilin merah. Di belakangnya, dua bayangan berselimut kabut mengejar; di bawah, air gelap menunggu. Kontras: kota hangat yang menjauh melawan abu-abu kematian jembatan dan dingin kabut.

## Prompt (siap tempel ke AI image generator)

```
A young Southeast Asian man in his mid-twenties, short dark hair, lean build, wearing a torn woven fiber courier jacket with a coiled rope around his left arm, running across a massive dead grey root bridge at dusk. Two sealed tubes hang at his waist, one with a red wax seal. Behind him, a glowing island-city with amber lights recedes into mist; two shadowy figures with curved knives emerge from fog in the far background. Below, dark still water fades to black. Motion blur on his legs, dust rising from dry roots. Light source: warm amber city glow from behind, cold blue-grey fog from ahead. Mood: chase, betrayal, determination. Color palette: charcoal grey, dried moss, amber gold, cold blue. Style: semi-realistic digital painting, dramatic perspective, fine root texture. No text on the image. Portrait orientation (3:4 aspect ratio). Influences: dark fantasy concept art meets Southeast Asian maritime aesthetic.
```

## Style

- **Genre**: Fantasi / Thriller
- **Mood**: terburu-buru, gelap, konspiratif
- **Art style**: digital painting semi-realistis, perspektif dramatis; bukan anime, bukan photorealistic
- **Lighting**: cahaya kota hangat di belakang (rim light) + kabut dingin di depan

## Color Palette

| Role | Hex | Description |
|------|-----|-------------|
| Background | `#3a3f3d` | jembatan mati dan kabut |
| Warm accent | `#d99a3d` | lampu Sarang dan segel lilin |
| Cold accent | `#5f7a86` | kabut dan air |
| Earth tone | `#6b5747` | serat jaket dan anyaman |
| Highlight | `#f0d9a0` | cahaya kota di tepi siluet |

## Negative Prompt (yang harus dihindari)

- Teks, judul, huruf, watermark
- Karakter chibi/cartoony
- Neon terang atau warna futuristik
- Elemen teknologi (mesin, kabel logam, kota modern)
- Komposisi statis — harus terasa bergerak

## Aspect Ratio

**3:4** (portrait) — standar cover novel.

## Catatan untuk Iterasi

- Jika jembatannya terlihat seperti akar hidup: buat abu-abu dan retak — kematian harus terbaca di teksturnya.
- Jika tokohnya terlihat terlalu Western: tambahkan ciri wajah Asia Tenggara, kulit sawo matang, rambut hitam.
- Detail yang paling sering salah: tabung serap terlihat seperti botol kaca — harus seperti ruas bambu/akar bersegel lilin.
