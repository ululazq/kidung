# Cover Prompt — The Thread Shop

## Concept
Toko benang tua di pasar malam yang redup, dengan benang-benang bercahaya yang menjahit realitas. Seorang perempuan muda (Rani) berdiri di depan rak benang, tangannya memegang seutas benang merah yang menyala, sementara benang-benang lain melayang membentuk pola tak kasat mata.

## Prompt
```
A moody, atmospheric digital painting of an old thread shop at night in a bustling night market. A young Indonesian woman (late 20s, short dark hair, wearing a simple modern jacket) stands before wooden shelves filled with spools of thread in every color. In her raised hand, a single glowing red thread unravels and weaves itself into shimmering patterns of light that connect to faint, ghostly silhouettes of people. Warm lantern light spills from the shop, contrasting with cool blue night shadows outside. Dust motes float in the light. Semi-realistic style, cinematic lighting, rich warm amber and deep teal palette. Portrait orientation, 3:4 aspect ratio. No text on the image.
```

## Style
- Genre: Contemporary urban fantasy
- Mood: Warm, mysterious, nostalgic
- Art style: Semi-realistic digital painting, cinematic
- Lighting: Warm lantern glow vs cool night shadows

## Color Palette
| Role | Hex | Description |
|------|-----|-------------|
| Primary | #E8A33D | Warm amber — lantern light, thread glow |
| Secondary | #1B2A41 | Deep teal-navy — night market shadows |
| Accent | #C0392B | Red — the glowing fate thread |
| Background | #2C1E16 | Dark warm brown — wooden shop interior |
| Highlight | #F5D9A0 | Soft cream — lantern highlights |

## Negative Prompt
No text, no watermark, no chibi, no neon glow, no cartoon, no anime, no extra fingers, no distorted faces, no busy background clutter.

## Aspect Ratio
3:4 (portrait)

## Catatan untuk Iterasi
- Jika benang terlalu samar, pertegas kontras merah vs latar gelap.
- Jika wajah Rani kurang jelas, minta close-up setengah badan.
- Jika pasar malam terlalu ramai, kurangi jumlah orang agar fokus ke toko.
