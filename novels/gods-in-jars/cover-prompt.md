# Gods in Jars — Cover Image Prompt

## Concept
Nadia berdiri di depan Toko Guci Malam yang gelap, memegang satu guci kecil yang bersinar lembut dari dalam. Di sekelilingnya, puluhan guci tanah liat berjajar di rak kayu tua. Asap dupa tipis mengalir di udara. Di belakang, siluet menara kaca Lupa Corp menjulang — kontras antara tradisi dan modernitas.

## Prompt (siap tempel ke AI image generator)

```
A dark, atmospheric book cover illustration. A young Indonesian woman (late 20s, short messy hair, wearing a simple dark green oversized shirt) stands in a dim, cluttered antique shop filled with clay jars on wooden shelves. She holds a small glowing jar close to her chest, emitting warm golden light. Wisps of incense smoke curl upward. Through the shop window behind her, a sleek glass corporate tower looms in the cold blue night. The shop is warm and earthy, the tower is cold and sterile — a visual contrast. Dark cozy atmosphere, muted earth tones with a single warm light source from the jar. No text on the image. Cinematic composition, portrait orientation (3:4 aspect ratio). Digital painting style, detailed, moody lighting. Influences: Studio Ghibli interiors meets Blade Runner 2049 lighting.
```

## Style
- **Genre**: Dark-cozy urban fantasy
- **Mood**: Hangat tapi suram. Ritualistic. Intimate.
- **Art style**: Digital painting, semi-realistic. Bukan anime, bukan photorealistic.
- **Lighting**: Remang dengan satu sumber cahaya hangat dari guci.

## Color Palette
| Role | Hex | Description |
|------|-----|-------------|
| Background | `#0d0f14` | Deep dark — langit malam / bayangan toko |
| Warm accent | `#c8956c` | Cahaya guci, dupa, tanah liat |
| Cold accent | `#4a7c9b` | Menara Lupa Corp, kaca, modernitas |
| Earth tone | `#6b4423` | Rak kayu, guci tanah liat, lantai |
| Smoke | `#8a8578` | Asap dupa, kabut tipis |

## Negative Prompt (yang harus dihindari)
- Teks, judul, huruf, watermark
- Karakter cartoony/chibi
- Warna-warna neon terang
- Background futuristik/sci-fi berlebihan
- Komposisi terlalu ramai — biarkan gelap dan kosong

## Aspect Ratio
**3:4** (portrait) — standar cover novel. Bisa juga 2:3.

## Catatan untuk Iterasi
- Jika hasil terlalu gelap: tambah sedikit rim light biru dari jendela
- Jika Nadia terlihat terlalu Western: tambahkan detail wajah Asia Tenggara, kulit sawo matang, rambut hitam
- Guci yang dipegang harus terlihat tanah liat — bukan kaca, bukan logam

## Lokasi File Cover
Setelah generate, simpan sebagai `public/covers/gods-in-jars.jpg` (atau `.png`).
