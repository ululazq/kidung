# Cover Prompt — The Astral Sovereign

### Concept
An intense, atmospheric dark urban fantasy book cover depicting an athletic Indonesian male protagonist in a rain-slicked futuristic Jakarta Sub-5 alley, holding a glowing ethereal bronze and obsidian glowing ring-blade emitting electric purple lightning and floating golden runes.

### Prompt
```
Dark urban action fantasy book cover featuring a young athletic Indonesian male in a rain-soaked futuristic cyberpunk Jakarta street at night, holding an intricate ancient bronze and obsidian gauntlet emitting bright purple astral lightning and glowing golden holographic runes, background filled with rainy neon-lit skyscrapers and dark metallic alleyways, cinematic lighting, dramatic gritty mood, semi-realistic digital painting, 3:4 portrait aspect ratio, no text on image.
```

### Style Specs
- Genre: Dark Urban Fantasy / Action Fantasy
- Mood: High-stakes, electric, gritty, cinematic
- Color Palette: Astral Violet (#8A2BE2), Obsidian Black (#0F0F14), Ancient Bronze (#CD7F32), Neon Gold (#FFD700)
- Aspect Ratio: 3:4 (Portrait)
