# Cover Prompt — The Shadow Weaver  

### Concept  
A dark urban fantasy book cover featuring a young Asian male weaver craftsman in a foggy rain-slicked Old Town Jakarta alley at night, with glowing golden shadow thread sigils and floating black silk threads swirling around his right arm and hand, dark misty atmosphere, cinematic lighting.

Action dark fantasy book cover featuring a young athletic Asian male weaver craftsman in a foggy rain-slicked cobble-paved Old Town Jakarta alley at night, wearing an intricate ancient black silk thread tattoo on his right forearm emitting glowing golden sparks and floating holographic gear runes, colonial Dutch heritage buildings in foggy background, cinematic dramatic lighting, dark atmospheric mood, semi-realistic digital painting, 3:4 aspect ratio, no text on the image.
