# Pustaka Kabut Senja — Cover Image Prompt

## Concept

Malam senja berdebu di lorong perpustakaan tua Kotatua Jakarta. Seorang pemuda (24 tahun, pemulih naskah kuno, kacamata perunggu, mantel kerja kulit cokelat tua) berdiri memegang kitab kulit kuno berukir Tinta Aetheric yang memancarkan pendaran keemasan murni. Di latar belakang, rak-rak kayu tua penuh gulungan kertas dan jendela tinggi bernuansa steampunk industrial.

## Prompt (siap tempel ke AI image generator)

```
A 24-year-old Southeast Asian male manuscript restorer with short black hair, wearing bronze round glasses and a dark brown leather artisan coat, holding an open ancient parchment book with glowing golden Aetheric ink runes. Standing inside a dusty Victorian-era library hall in Old Jakarta, surrounding towering wooden bookshelves filled with leather-bound manuscripts and rolled scrolls. Twilight sunlight filtering through high arched glass windows, warm amber and golden lighting, atmospheric dust motes in the air, cinematic composition, semi-realistic digital painting, dark folk urban fantasy mood. No text on the image. Portrait orientation (3:4 aspect ratio).
```

## Style

- **Genre**: Folk Urban Fantasy / Mystery
- **Mood**: Atmosferik, redup, misterius, beraroma tinta tua
- **Art style**: Digital painting semi-realistis
- **Lighting**: Cahaya senja keemasan menembus jendela tinggi
