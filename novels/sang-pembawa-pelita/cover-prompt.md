# Cover Prompt — Sang Pembawa Pelita

### Concept
A dark steampunk urban fantasy book cover featuring a young athletic Asian male courier holding an ancient glowing brass lantern emitting warm golden light and floating holographic shield runes in a foggy rain-slicked industrial Bawah-Batavia shipping dock at night, cinematic lighting, dark atmospheric mood.

### Prompt
```
Action steampunk book cover featuring a young athletic Asian male courier in a foggy rain-slicked cobble-paved Neo-Batavia shipping dock at night, holding an intricate ancient brass and copper lantern in his hand emitting glowing golden aetheric light and floating holographic shield runes, cargo cranes and steam pipes in foggy background, cinematic dramatic lighting, dark atmospheric mood, semi-realistic digital painting, 3:4 aspect ratio, no text on the image.
```
