# Cover Prompt — The Iron Karma

### Concept
A high-voltage urban fantasy action cover showing an athletic Indonesian protagonist wearing an intricate ancient metallic gauntlet glowing with lightning sparks in a dark rainy Jakarta alley.

### Prompt
Action fantasy book cover featuring a young Indonesian male athletic courier in a dark wet rain-slicked Jakarta alley at night, wearing a glowing ancient metallic cybernetic gauntlet on his right arm emitting blue lightning sparks, neon city reflections on wet asphalt, cinematic lighting, gritty urban mood, digital painting style, 3:4 aspect ratio, no text on the image.

- Art Style: Semi-realistic digital painting, dramatic cinematic lighting
